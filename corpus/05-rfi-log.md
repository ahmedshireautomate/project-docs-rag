# Riverside Health Centre, Level 1: RFI Log (Finishes Package)

Document ref: RHC-RFI-LOG, issue 7.

## RFI-009: Paint to plasterboard ceiling margins

Raised by Northgate Decorating on 3 March. Question: the specification is silent on the colour of the plasterboard ceiling margins. Response from the architect, Hollis Marr Architects, on 6 March: margins are PT-1 in white, as now stated in specification clause 09.5 at revision C. Status: closed. No cost or programme effect.

## RFI-014: Wall finish in Staff Shower (Room 1.06)

Raised by Northgate Decorating on 17 March. Question: the schedule shows WC-1 cladding to 2100 mm with PT-2 above in the Staff Shower, but the room has no mechanical extract above the cladding line and the painted band will be exposed to steam. Response from the architect on 21 March: WC-1 is to run full height to the underside of the ceiling in Room 1.06 only. The Accessible WC (Room 1.05) and Dirty Utility (Room 1.04) remain as specified, with WC-1 to 2100 mm and PT-2 above. Status: closed. Cost effect: variation VO-006 agreed at 412 pounds. No programme effect.

## RFI-017: Spray application of PT-2

Raised by Northgate Decorating on 28 March. Question: can PT-2 be spray applied to the Treatment Room to save time? Response from the architect on 30 March: not accepted. Clause 09.2 stands. PT-2 is brush and roller only because the adjacent ground floor clinic remains in use and overspray and odour cannot be controlled. Status: closed.

## RFI-021: Moisture readings in Consulting Room B

Raised by the site manager, Dana Whitlock, on 9 April. Question: the north wall of Room 1.02 is reading 19 percent wood moisture equivalent after the leak from the Level 2 riser on 2 April. Can decoration proceed? Response from the architect on 10 April: no. Clause 09.3 requires 16 percent or below. Run dehumidifiers and re-test every 48 hours. Decoration to the north wall of Room 1.02 is on hold until two consecutive readings are at or below 16 percent. Status: open. Programme effect: up to 5 working days to Room 1.02 only.

## RFI-023: Feature wall covering lead time

Raised by Northgate Decorating on 14 April. Question: WC-2 has a 4 week lead time from order, and the pattern has not been approved. Response from the architect on 16 April: pattern reference "Linden 04" is approved. Order immediately. Status: closed. The waiting area feature wall is now the last finishes activity on the programme.
