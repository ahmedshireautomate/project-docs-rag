# Riverside Health Centre, Level 1 Fit-Out: Finishes Specification (Section 09)

Document ref: RHC-SPEC-09 rev C. SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written for demonstration. The project, people and companies are fictional.

## 09.1 Scope

This section covers painting, wall coverings and resilient flooring to Level 1 of Riverside Health Centre. Ceilings are covered in Section 09.5. External decoration is excluded from this contract.

## 09.2 Paint systems

PT-1 is a water-based acrylic eggshell for general walls. Apply one mist coat thinned 10 percent followed by two full coats. Minimum dry film thickness is 60 microns across the two full coats.

PT-2 is a water-based anti-microbial hygiene coating for clinical rooms. Apply one coat of the manufacturer's primer followed by two full coats. Minimum dry film thickness is 80 microns. PT-2 must be applied by brush and roller only; spray application is not permitted inside the building.

PT-3 is a water-based satin for timber doors, frames and skirtings. Apply one primer coat, one undercoat and one finishing coat.

All paints must have a VOC content below 30 g/l. Colours are given in the Room Finish Schedule (RHC-SCH-09).

## 09.3 Substrate preparation

New plasterboard must be taped and jointed to a Level 4 finish before decoration. Moisture content of plastered walls must be at or below 1 percent by carbide meter, or at or below 16 percent wood moisture equivalent by pin meter, before any coating is applied.

Fill all surface defects greater than 1 mm. Sand, dust off and spot prime filler before the mist coat.

## 09.4 Wall coverings

WC-1 is a 2 mm PVC hygienic wall cladding sheet for wet rooms and the dirty utility, fixed with the manufacturer's two-part adhesive. Joints are to be thermo-welded. Sheets run from the top of the coved skirting to 2100 mm above finished floor level; the wall above is painted PT-2.

WC-2 is a fabric-backed vinyl wall covering, 350 g/m2, used in the waiting area feature wall only. Allow 10 percent waste for pattern matching.

## 09.5 Ceilings

Ceilings are mineral fibre tiles in an exposed grid and are not painted, except the plasterboard margins, which receive PT-1 in white.

## 09.6 Resilient flooring

FL-1 is a 2 mm homogeneous sheet vinyl with welded joints and a 100 mm coved skirting, used in all clinical rooms. FL-2 is a carpet tile used in offices and the staff room. The screed relative humidity must be at or below 75 percent before FL-1 is laid.

## 09.7 Measurement rules for take-off

Wall areas are measured net. Deduct all openings larger than 0.5 m2. Do not deduct openings of 0.5 m2 or smaller. Measure wall height from finished floor level to the underside of the suspended ceiling plus 100 mm. Reveals deeper than 100 mm are measured separately. Allow 5 percent waste on paint and 10 percent on WC-2.

## 09.8 Tolerances and acceptance

Painted surfaces are inspected from 1.5 m in normal lighting. There must be no visible brush marks, runs, misses or grinning of the substrate. Colour must match the approved 1 m2 sample panel for each paint system.
